# Architecture decision records

Load the `adr` skill before writing or changing an ADR. Files are
`docs/decisions/ADR-NNNN-kebab-slug.md`, numbered in sequence and never reused or renumbered.
An accepted ADR is not rewritten: a change of mind is a new ADR that supersedes it.
